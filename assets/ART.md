# claude-house art

The mascot is Hermi (小寄): a coral hermit crab whose shell is a small cream cottage with terracotta tiles, a warm round window, chimney smoke and a roof sprout.

## Where the art came from

- The illustrated pictures (brand images, icon masters, avatar, wallpapers, sign-in screen and terminal background) were made with an AI image generation tool, with `brand/mascot-sheet.png` as the character reference. The exact prompt for each picture is in the table below and in `tools/generation-records.json`.
- The avatar is reduced to four flat colours. The sign-in screen and the two wallpapers share one fixed camera on the same cottage: at sunset the round door is shut and nobody is visible; in the morning Hermi comes out and waves; at dusk he sits on the doorstep with a mug.
- The cursors and the 16 and 24 px house icons are drawn in code by `tools/make_cursors.py`.
- Pillow does every resize, the ICO, CUR and ANI conversions, and the proof sheets.
- Nothing in this folder installs anything or changes Windows. Step 13 of the runbook applies the art inside the guest.

The animated cursors have 12 unique frames and 60 playback steps. Windows stores ANI timing in 1/60-second units, so alternating 4- and 5-unit steps give an exact 80 ms average; an exact 80 ms for every single frame cannot be stored.

## Rebuild

- `py -3 -B assets/tools/finish_art.py` (needs Pillow) resizes the generated pictures to their target sizes, redraws the cursors and icons, draws the proof sheets, validates every file and rewrites this page.
- `py -3 -B assets/tools/w13b_scene.py` (needs Pillow, numpy, opencv-python and the Microsoft YaHei Bold font) rebuilds only the sign-in screen, the two wallpapers and `preview/lockscreen-mock.png`. It reads the three native 1672 × 941 generations listed in `tools/w13b-inputs.json`, by default from `tools/native/`. Those files are not in the repository; generate them again with the prompts in that file.
- `py -3 -B assets/tools/w13_review.py before|after` records cursor and icon measurements in `tools/w13-verification.json`; `py -3 -B assets/tools/w13_review.py proof` redraws `preview/w13-checks.png`.
- To use your own picture, replace the file at the same path. Step 13 uses `look/wallpaper.png`, `look/lockscreen.png` and `look/avatar.png` as they are; see `look/README.md`.
